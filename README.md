### 👋 Hey, I'm Nikola

By day I'm a **QA Engineer at [Allied Testing](https://alliedtesting.com)**, building Java test automation for backend services.
In my free time I design and build software: cross-platform desktop apps with **Tauri** (Rust + TypeScript), web apps and AI integrations.

I study Computer Science at the **University of Niš**. Testing for a living makes me a pickier builder, and building makes me a better tester.

- 🧪 &nbsp;Currently automating backend test suites in **Java**
- 🦀 &nbsp;After hours: **Rust**, **Tauri** and **React**, plus on-device AI with **llama.cpp**
- 🎨 &nbsp;Designing everything I ship in **Figma**
- 💬 &nbsp;Open to side projects, [let's talk](https://nikolchaa.com)
